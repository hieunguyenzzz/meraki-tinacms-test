    description_en: |
      Claire & Luis came to us with a clear vision from the beginning: to find a space that felt warm, intimate, and deeply rooted in cultural character. That vision naturally led us to Lan Viên Cổ Tích, a quiet garden house in the heart of Huế where antique ceramics, weathered wooden structures, and carefully preserved details carried the beauty of a home shaped gently across generations.

      Because the venue was first and foremost a family home and cultural space, hosting a private wedding there came with its own challenges. Together with the venue team, we carefully shaped the celebration in a way that felt both intimate and respectful to the spirit of the house itself. Rather than transforming the space completely, we chose to let its existing soul lead the story, gently weaving contemporary details into the old charm of Huế throughout the celebration.
