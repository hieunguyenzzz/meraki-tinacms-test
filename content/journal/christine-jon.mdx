      As golfers, Christie and Jonathan had already decided before the planning even began: their wedding had to take place on a golf course.

With the ceremony set deeper into the course, transportation became an important part of the planning, with guests arriving by golf carts and each transition carefully timed to keep the journey smooth and effortless.
