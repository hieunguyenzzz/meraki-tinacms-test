      What we loved most about Cam and Tony’s celebration was the sense of family woven through every part of the weekend. Their love story has never been quite conventional, they met at very different stages of life, brought two families together, welcomed four children, and first made their marriage official through an intimate elopement in Las Vegas during COVID. Five years later, they chose to celebrate once again, this time bringing their children, family, and closest friends together in Vietnam, a country they both love. More than a destination wedding, the celebration became a way of sharing the place with the people who mattered most to them and creating a new chapter together as a family.

Từ đó, tụi mình cùng mở rộng bảng màu, thêm vào những sắc hoa tươi sáng và nhiều chuyển động hơn để không gian trở nên vui vẻ, nhẹ nhàng và có sức sống.
