    description_en: |
      Red & Blue drew from the vivid colours of Vietnamese weddings of the past.

      For their vow ceremony, Sarah & Matthieu chose to wear áo dài, bringing a distinctly Vietnamese touch into a moment more commonly associated with Western traditions. Sarah wore red, while Matthieu wore blue, with the two colours reflecting their contrasting personalities and becoming a thread woven throughout the celebration.

      They appeared again in the stationery and keepsakes prepared for their guests, from engraved bamboo chopsticks and traditional fans to small magnets featuring the couple in red and blue. Two lions, representing Sarah and Matthieu, also became a playful part of the celebration.

      Many of their guests arrived in áo dài as well, allowing these familiar cultural touches to come together naturally throughout the day.
