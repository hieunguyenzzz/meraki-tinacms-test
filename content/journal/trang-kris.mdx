    description_en: |
      In contrast to the bride's garden, the groom's home embraced the quiet elegance of its architecture, with warm brown walls and clean, understated lines. Rather than transforming the space, we allowed its existing character to guide every design decision.

      With the ceremony taking place just before Lunar New Year, we embraced the philosophy of celebrating what each season naturally offers. Snow apricot blossoms, dahlias, budding plum branches, and the most beautiful flowers of the season became the heart of the floral concept. Arranged in the spirit of Ikebana, each composition preserved the natural beauty of every stem while complementing the home's calm minimalism. Like the first gentle signs of spring, the arrangements quietly settled into every corner of the house, bringing with them the warmth and anticipation of family reunions just around the corner.
