    description_en: |
      After more than seven years together, Thư and Minh had shared a journey filled with adventures, growth, and countless memories. For their wedding day, we wanted to bring that same sense of gentleness into every detail.

      Soft pastel blooms set the tone throughout the celebration, from the bridal bouquet to the ceremony arch and floral arrangements. Orchids, roses, chrysanthemums, and baby’s breath came together in shades of yellow, peach, pink, lavender, and soft blue, creating a palette that felt unmistakably spring.

      Transparent elements were incorporated into both the guest seating and ceremony backdrop, allowing the flowers and natural light to take center stage. As sunlight passed through the structures, subtle reflections from the surrounding blooms created an almost dreamlike ombré effect, making the space feel as though it had been lifted from a garden in full bloom.

      At the heart of the design stood a transparent rectangular arch adorned with delicate flowers. From afar, it resembled a soft rainbow suspended in the air, or perhaps a picture frame quietly capturing one of the most meaningful moments of Thư and Minh’s story.
