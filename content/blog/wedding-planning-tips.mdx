      Where it all began

      What was it about your partner that made you fall in love?

      Maybe it was something big. Or maybe it was something small like the way they laugh, the way they listen, the way they make ordinary days feel lighter.

      Go back to that moment when you knew.

      And start from there.

      How love has changed you

      Love doesn’t just bring two people together, it shapes them.

      Think about who you were before, and who you’ve become since.

      What has this relationship taught you?

      How has it softened you, challenged you, or helped you grow?

      This is often the part that makes people feel most connected to your story because it’s real.

      ***What you promise***

      Vows are, at their heart, promises. Not grand, perfect promises but honest ones.

      Maybe it’s about being there on difficult days. Maybe it’s about building a home that feels safe. Maybe it’s simply about choosing each other, again and again.

      The simpler it is, the more it stays.

      ***The life you imagine together***

      There’s something beautiful about speaking your future out loud.

      The trips you’ve talked about. The small routines you look forward to. Even the silly plans that only the two of you understand.

      These details make your vows feel alive, not just meaningful, but yours.
