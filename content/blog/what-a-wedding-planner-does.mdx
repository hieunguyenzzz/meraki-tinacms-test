      ### 1. A Vendor ***CONSULTANT***

      How do you find vendors among a plethora of choices on the Internet? As vendors such as decorators, make-up artists, or photographers are essential in every wedding, a wedding planner will search and connect you to the right person. They look for vendors, coordinate with them, and harmonize everyone into one ultimate goal, which is making your big day as memorable as possible.

      3\.
      A Checklist
      **MANAGER**

      There are so many details that need to be done, from prepping for photoshoots to try out wedding dresses, booking restaurants, scouting for wedding venues, finding music bands, etc. Diving into those tasks without thinking of priorities may get you overwhelmed. Therefore, a wedding planner will help you with a checklist to have a picture of the wedding. They will also remind and keep you on track with the planning.

Khi ấy, Wedding Planner là người đảm nhiệm vai trò này, và bạn cùng người bạn đời chỉ cần tận hưởng ngày vui của mình một cách thật trọn vẹn.
