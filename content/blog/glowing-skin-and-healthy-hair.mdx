      ## PRE-WEDDING SKINCARE TIPS

      Sebum excretion proceeds through the night, so you should prime your skin by cleansing it for your morning skincare routine.       ## WHAT ARE THE BEST FRUITS FOR A HEALTHY SKIN?

      Banana, avocado, apple, etc. are fruits that are rich in vitamins and high in antioxidants. Besides, vegetables like tomato, broccoli, kale and nuts also boost your skin's appearance and give it a glowy look. And drink plenty of water for a youthful, plump, and clear skin.

      ## WHAT ARE THE FOODS THAT ARE BAD FOR YOUR SKIN?

      Avoid foods that are high in saturated fats, foods that contain oil such as fried foods, or food with a high amount of sugar. Furthermore, alcohol and tobacco can cause bad effects for your skin as well.

      Milk and yogurt are good foods for the skin. However, cheeses, ice cream, milk scum, and caramel should be limited because they contain a high amount of progesterone and steroids, which could possibly increase overactive fat glands and cause acne.

      ## Hair

      ## SHOULD I WASH MY HAIR A DAY BEFORE THE WEDDING?

      Always wash your hair the night before the wedding. However, for some certain hairstyles, slightly dirty hair does give a great texture to make it easier to style. So, talk to your hairstylist before the wedding to clarify this manner.

      Try not to use too much conditioner, or skip it if your hair is not too dry. Likewise, if you have oily hair, avoid using conditioner for the heavy and greasy look it would give your hair. Keep in mind that some conditioners can ruin the hair texture, making it difficult to style your hair.

      ## SHOULD I DETOX MY HAIR?

      Hair detoxing allows you to get rid of all excess minerals that block the pores on your scalp. You can also find good hair detox products on the market or just simply do it at home with apple cider vinegar.

      ## SIMPLE HAIR DETOX RECIPE

      **What you need:**
      60ml of apple cider vinegar and 480ml of water.
      How to do it:
      Step 1: Wash and condition your hair as usual
      Step 2: Mix apple cider vinegar with water.
      Step 3: Gently rub the mixture on your hair and leave it for 3 to 5 minutes
      Step 4: Rinse again with water.

      If you want to trim your hair, do it at least a week before your wedding. On the other hand, to change your hairstyle, you should do it at least 2-4 weeks before the wedding date.

Choosing the right hairstyle depends on many factors, your face type, type of your hair, or the vibe of the celebration.
